## Discord

Join the [BrowserAct Discord community](https://discord.com/invite/UpnCKd7GaU) and create a support ticket.

## Email

Email [support@browseract.com](mailto:support@browseract.com).

## What to Include

For a product issue, include the Bot ID, Task ID, target URL, expected behavior, actual behavior, and a screenshot with sensitive information removed.

For an invoice request, include the order information needed to identify the purchase.

Never send passwords, verification codes, credential secrets, or full payment-card details.
