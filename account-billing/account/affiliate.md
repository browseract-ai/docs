Join the [BrowserAct Affiliate Program](https://www.browseract.com/affiliate) to create a permanent referral link, share BrowserAct with your audience, and earn commission from eligible purchases.

## Current Affiliate Offer

- Earn **30% commission** on a referred user's first order.
- Earn **20% commission** on that user's purchases during the following six months.
- Track clicks, registrations, purchases, and commission in the affiliate dashboard.
- Receive eligible payouts on a monthly schedule.
- There is no stated cap on total commission earnings.

## How to Get Started

1. Open the [BrowserAct Affiliate Program](https://www.browseract.com/affiliate).
2. Select `Get Started Now` and register for the program.
3. Get your referral link from the affiliate dashboard.
4. Share the link through tutorials, articles, communities, social posts, templates, or client recommendations.
5. Track referrals and eligible commission in the dashboard.

Affiliate terms, commission rates, attribution rules, and payout requirements may change. Review the current program page and affiliate dashboard before promoting BrowserAct.
