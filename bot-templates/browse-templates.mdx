Open the [BrowserAct Template Marketplace](https://www.browseract.com/template?co-from=bot-template-help-center) to find ready-to-run Bots.

## Choose a Template

1. Search for the website or task you want to automate.
2. Open a template to review its purpose, required inputs, expected output, approximate credit range, and supported Browser Settings.
3. Select `Quick Start` when the template already matches your scenario.
4. Select `Create Editable Duplicate` when you need to change its fields, logic, or target scenario.

Approximate credits are provided as a reference. Actual usage may vary by website, input size, browser settings, and runtime behavior.
