---

Account Credentials supports Bots built with Agent or Workflow. Add credentials from Account Credentials, then authorize the Bots that need them. Credentials created through a Bot's Website login settings are automatically authorized for that Bot.

Follow [Use account credentials](/website-login/account-credentials?co-from=link-Docs) to add account details, set up supported authenticator-app 2FA, authorize or revoke Bot access, and manage credentials. The guide includes the credential forms and examples.

For email or SMS verification, use [Manual login](/website-login/manual-login?co-from=link-Docs), or import cookies from a verified session. Keep passwords and authenticator secret keys out of Build prompts and support messages.
