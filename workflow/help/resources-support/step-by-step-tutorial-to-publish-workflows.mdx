## ✅ **1. Open the Creator Plan Page**

1. Log in to your [**BrowserAct Dashboard.**](https://www.browseract.com/reception)
2. From the left menu, click **“Creator Plan”.** You’ll see the **application form** here.

![image.png](/images/image.png)

---

## ✅ **2. Apply to Become a Creator**

1. Fill in the required details:
   1. Your **name** and **email address**
   2. **Social media links** (e.g., Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn)
   3. A short summary of what kind of workflows or content you create
1. Click **“Submit Application.”**
2. The BrowserAct team will review your submission within **48 hours** and notify you by email once it’s approved.

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-13-45.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-13-45.png)

---

## ✅ **3. Access Publish Management**

- Once your application is approved, **“Creator Plan”** in the sidebar will automatically change to **“Publish Management.”**
- This is your **creator dashboard**, where you can manage and publish all of your workflow templates.

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-14-11.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-14-11.png)

---

## ✅ **4. Choose What to Publish**

Inside **Publish Management**, you’ll see two publishing options:

| **Option**                    | **Description**                         |
| :---------------------------: | :-------------------------------------: |
| **Publish from Workflow**     | Publish a BrowserAct workflow directly  |
| **Publish Make/n8n Template** | Publish a Make or n8n workflow template |

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-14-35.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-14-35.png)

---

## ✅ **5. Publish a BrowserAct Workflow**

1. Click **“Publish from Workflow.”**
2. Choose an existing workflow from the list or create a new one.

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-15-15.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-15-15.png)

Fill in the details — template name, category, tags, and a short description.

4. Click **“Submit Review.”**

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-15-52.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-15-52.png)

---

## ✅ **6. Publish a Make or n8n Workflow**

1. Click **“Publish Other Template.”**

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-16-26.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-16-26.png)

2. Fill in the form with the following details:
   1. **Template name** and **cover image**
   2. Select the **platform** (Make or n8n)
   3. Upload the **required JSON file**
   4. Add a **workflow diagram** or **template URL** for reference
   5. Complete **category**, **tags**, and **description** fields
1. Click **“Submit.”** Review usually takes up to **48 hours.**

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-16-47.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-16-47.png)

💡 **Tip:**  To make your workflow usable directly inside **Make** or **n8n**,  be sure to **link the BrowserAct workflow** used within it under **“Related BrowserAct Templates.”**  Only **BrowserAct workflows that have already been published** can be linked.

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-17-06.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-17-06.png)

---

## ✅ **7. Complete Your Creator Profile**

1. Click the ✏️ icon next to your avatar to open **Profile Settings.**

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-17-40.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-17-40.png)

2. Upload a profile picture, add your social media links, and write a short bio.
3. Click **Submit for Review.**

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-18-00.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-18-00.png)

4. Once approved, your profile will appear on your **public creator page.**

---

## ✅ **8. View Your Creator Page**

After your profile and templates are approved,  BrowserAct will automatically generate a **public creator page** showing:

- Your avatar and bio
- All your published templates
- Direct links for other users to view and use your workflows

![PixPin_2025-12-12_17-18-21.png](/images/PixPin_2025-12-12_17-18-21.png)

---

## ✅ **9. Rewards and Earnings**

- **Welcome Bonus** — Earn **10,000 credits** for your first published template.
- **Weekly Reward Pool** — Join the **\$500 cash reward pool** every week.
- Earn more credits as your templates are used and gain visibility through the community.
