### Get Help

**Online support:**

- [Contact BrowserAct online support](https://www.browseract.com/?support=messages&co-from=link-Docs).
- If the panel does not open automatically, select the customer support button and open **Messages**.

**Email:**

- 📧 [service@browseract.com](mailto:service@browseract.com)
- Response: Within 48 hours
- Best for: Complex issues with screenshots

**Include this info:**

- Screenshots or error codes
- Methods you've already tried
- Workflow ID or Task ID
